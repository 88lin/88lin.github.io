Finished writing, but still adjusting fonts, colors, and spacing? **Give the draft to AI and let it handle the layout.** Choose a style you like, and it will arrange headings, introductions, highlights, images, and your signature. Open the preview, copy the result, and continue editing in WeChat.

<a id="quick-start"></a>

## 🚀 The easiest way: copy this prompt and send it to AI

**Start here — let AI help with installation.** Click the copy button at the top right of the code box, send the whole prompt to your AI, then give it your article:

```text
Please install this WeChat layout tool:
https://github.com/88lin/gzh-skill-kit

Format my article for WeChat.
Choose a theme and preserve my content.
Check the layout and create a preview
with a Copy to WeChat button.
Show me how to open it and copy.
Help me install anything else it needs.
```

> [!TIP]
> **You provide the article; AI helps with setup and layout.** Use an AI client that can install tools and work with files, such as Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor. A basic web chat may not support this; see the [FAQ](#faq).

No draft ready? Send this next:

> Try the included sample article first so I can see the result.

**📄 Send an article → 🎨 AI formats it → 📋 Open the preview and copy → 📱 Check it in WeChat**

## ✨ What it helps you do

- **🎨 Find a style**: 14 ready-made themes, from clear tutorials and magazine features to warm reading journals. Ask AI to recommend one if you are unsure.
- **🪄 Arrange the page**: section numbering, introductions, contents lists, keyword highlights, and author signatures.
- **📄 Use the draft you have**: paste text or provide Markdown, Word, or PDF. Other formats are organized into Markdown before layout.
- **🖼️ Bring text and images together**: images, GIFs, quotes, lists, tables, and code blocks, with a clear reading structure for long articles.
- **🧠 Create your own look**: describe a style or share a reference image, then reuse the resulting theme for future articles.
- **📋 Copy and check**: get a preview with a copy button and checks for common layout problems, ready for your final review in WeChat.

## 📖 Walk through your first article

### 📄 Step 1: give AI your article

Once installed, paste your text or share a file. For example:

```text
Format this article for WeChat and choose a suitable theme.
Keep my content, use "Your Name" for the author signature,
and give me a preview I can open.
```

Writing in Word is fine too:

> Format this Word document. Check that its sections and images are complete, then use the Letter theme.

PDFs can also be organized before layout. Scanned pages, complex tables, and multi-column content may need your review. For plain text without headings, AI can suggest a section structure.

### 🎨 Step 2: pick a style, or let AI choose

Name a theme directly, or compare two versions first:

> Use Journal for these reading notes, keeping my quotes and annotations.

> Make Tech Cobalt and Letter versions so I can compare them.

> Choose the theme that fits the article and finish the layout without asking me to pick.

If you are unsure, start with the default **Tech Cobalt**. More suggestions are in the [theme guide](#themes) below.

### 📋 Step 3: open the preview and copy to WeChat

1. **Open the preview from AI**: its filename usually ends in `_预览.html`.
2. **Click 复制到公众号 (Copy to WeChat)** at the top right to copy the formatted article.
3. **Paste into the article body in WeChat**: `Ctrl+V` on Windows or `⌘V` on Mac.
4. **Preview it on your phone**: check images, headings, and the author signature before publishing.

> [!TIP]
> If AI gives you two HTML files, open the one with `预览` (preview) in its name. The other is the article file used for checks and upload; you do not need to edit its code.

### ✏️ Step 4: tell AI what you would like to change

Describe the part you want adjusted:

> There are too many highlights in section two. Use fewer and leave more space.

> Keep the content and sections, but switch the article to Magazine.

> Show me which sentences you plan to emphasize before generating the layout.

For a style you can keep using:

```text
Create a theme for my reading newsletter: cream paper, dark green headings,
and quotes that look like notes in the margin of a book. Keep it warm and clear.
Show me a preview first, then save it after I confirm so I can reuse it.
```

## 📮 Prefer sending the article straight to your draft box?

The default route is **preview → copy → paste**, with no WeChat API setup. If you publish regularly and want fewer manual steps, ask AI to help configure draft upload:

```text
I want to send the formatted article to my WeChat draft box.
First check whether my Official Account has the required API permissions
and guide me through local configuration. Once configured, upload a draft.
I will preview it and confirm publication in WeChat myself.
```

This requires your account's API permissions and credentials. **The tool creates drafts; you decide when to publish.** Give AI the [📮 draft upload guide](docs/advanced.en.md#wechat-api) for the setup details.

## ❓ FAQ

**Can I use it without writing code?**

Follow the default route: copy the prompt, send AI your article, then open the preview and copy. AI helps with the technical setup and layout steps; you review the content and final appearance.

**Which AI should I send the prompt to?**

Use a client that can install skills, read files, and run tools, such as Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor. A basic web chat may lack these abilities. If unsure, send the starter prompt and ask it to check whether your current environment supports the tool.

**Do I need to learn Markdown first?**

No. Paste your article or supply Word, PDF, or plain text. Save older `.doc` files as `.docx` in Word or WPS first. Complex PDF figures and scanned content need extra review.

**Why do local images disappear after pasting into WeChat?**

WeChat cannot read images directly from your computer's file paths. Upload them in the editor, or use the draft upload flow to process local article images.

**Will the pasted result look exactly like the preview?**

The tool follows WeChat's layout constraints and checks the output, but the editor and different phones can display some details differently. Use WeChat's mobile preview before publishing. Give AI a screenshot and the source article if you need adjustments.

**How do I update it or get help with an error?**

Ask AI to help, keeping your custom themes when updating. For example:

> Update gzh-skill-kit, preserve the themes I added or changed, and check that it works with the sample article.

Still stuck? [Report the issue](https://github.com/88lin/gzh-skill-kit/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) with the AI client, theme name, and a screenshot.
